Project Overview

The finish you see, the precision we deliver. A bold fit-out brought to life with purpose.

​For the Madagascar-themed activation at Dubai Festival City Mall, Eventbox collaborated with us to deliver a complete custom vehicle fit-out. From the initial technical structural engineering to the final steel fabrication, wood paneling, professional painting, and branding sticker application, every element was crafted to transform a standard golf cart into an immersive, high-impact display.
