ZIAD RAPHAEL NASSAR WEDDINGS

Royal Wedding | Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Project Overview

A breathtaking, bespoke luxury wedding space constructed in Riyadh, showcasing unparalleled craftsmanship and royal grandeur.

RR2Global partnered with renowned wedding designer Ziad Raphael Nassar to engineer and build this high-end celebratory venue. Every element, from the custom ceiling installations to the grand floral backdrops and intricate lighting layouts, was fabricated and assembled with absolute precision.

Using premium materials, sophisticated structural engineering, and seamless integration of immersive stage design, our team transformed the venue into an exquisite fairytale environment that set a new standard for luxury events in Saudi Arabia.
