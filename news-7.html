Two New Successes

The Madagascar Experience & Sharjah Padel Day Event

Latest Project Updates

We are thrilled to announce the successful completion and launch of two of our most recent major projects, showcasing our versatility across immersive brand activations and large-scale corporate events.

The Madagascar Experience (Dubai Festival City Mall)

In partnership with Event Box, RR2Global designed and built a breathtaking custom-themed play and adventure park to bring the beloved Madagascar franchise to life. This brand activation featured vibrant character models, engaging obstacle courses, and highly interactive activity hubs. The precision in physical modeling and spatial design ensured children and families were entirely immersed in an unforgettable experience.

Invest in Sharjah Business & Padel Day

Shifting gears to corporate engagement, we successfully managed and executed the "Invest in Sharjah Business & Padel Day Event" at the World Padel Academy (WPA) in Al Rahmaniya, Sharjah. Organized for our esteemed client OUNCE, this event combined high-level business networking with an energetic Padel tournament. From bespoke staging and lighting to seamless attendee flow, RR2Global ensured a flawless corporate experience.

"Whether it is an immersive adventure park for families or a high-stakes corporate networking event, our commitment to Digital Craftsmanship remains the same."

These two projects underscore our team's adaptability and creative vision. We invite you to explore the full galleries of these events on our Projects page, and stay tuned for more exciting builds coming soon!
