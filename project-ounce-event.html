Project Overview

Innovation, branding expertise, and a dedication to detail—our recipe for another successful corporate event.

At the Wasl Experience Center, we delivered the design, production, and installation for the Avenue Park Towers Launch Event, organized by The Name Agency. From architectural display structures and scenic branding walls to immersive stage and presentation environments, every element was crafted to highlight the development in a refined and engaging setting.

From the stage to the reception counter detail, booths, and overall brand integration, every element was seamlessly branded to create a unified and professional event presence. A corporate experience engineered for impact. We don't do ordinary.
