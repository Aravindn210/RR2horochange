INVEST IN SHARJAH
BUSINESS & GOLF DAY

Corporate Event | Sharjah, United Arab Emirates

Project Overview

A premium corporate networking and sporting event setup designed and executed in partnership with Ounce Events, combining professional business environments with a luxury golf day experience.

RR2Global engineered and managed the entire event infrastructure, including customized branded installations, premium spectator setups, registration counters, and interactive outdoor brand touchpoints designed to engage high-profile guests.

Through bespoke structural fabrication, high-resolution signage, and premium outdoor furnishing, the team delivered an exceptional, high-impact environment that seamlessly blended professional networking with the spirit of golf.

Project Brief

  • Category Corporate Event
  • Location Sharjah, United Arab Emirates
  • Completion December 2025
