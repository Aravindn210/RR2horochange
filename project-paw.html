PAW PATROL

BRAND ACTIVATION

Project Overview

Where adventure meets imagination.

At Dubai Festival City Mall, we delivered the production and installation for Event Box’s PAW Patrol activation, transforming the outdoor space into a vibrant world inspired by the beloved characters.

From large-scale themed structures and scenic builds to interactive play zones and immersive environments, every element was carefully fabricated and installed to create an engaging experience for families and young fans. A playful city brought to life—designed for exploration, fun, and unforgettable moments.
