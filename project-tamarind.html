TRILEGAL
25TH ANNIVERSARY EVENT

Corporate Event | Yas Beach, Abu Dhabi, UAE

Project Overview

A stunning beachside corporate celebration designed and executed at Yas Beach, Abu Dhabi, in collaboration with Tamarind Global to celebrate Trilegal's 25th Anniversary.

RR2Global engineered the structural layout and stage construction directly on the beach, blending luxury hospitality setups with grand event production. Deliverables included customized staging, dynamic outdoor lighting networks, branding installations, and bespoke seating layouts tailored for an exclusive coastal atmosphere.

Despite challenging seaside outdoor conditions, the technical execution and meticulous finishing ensured a flawless corporate experience that beautifully celebrated Trilegal's milestone anniversary.

Project Brief

  • Location Yas Beach, Abu Dhabi, UAE
